# Manafa Digital Business Onboarding Presentation

## Purpose

This document defines the reusable company and Digital Business onboarding presentation for Business Analyst and UX/UI co-op joiners.

- **Audience:** BA and UX/UI co-ops, commonly from MIS or IS backgrounds
- **Owner:** Product Director, Digital Business, or a delegated team member
- **Language:** English first
- **Format:** Interactive 16:9 HTML presentation
- **Duration:** Approximately 60–90 minutes, depending on discussion
- **Scope:** Company orientation, business model, products, organization, Digital Business, platforms, delivery workflow, team and essential tools
- **Visual direction:** Manafa Weekly Product Leadership presentation system, using official logos, portraits, product icons and approved photographs

The presentation is intentionally separate from the Buddy Program and the seven-month development journey. It explains the environment the joiner is entering; the Buddy Program explains how a colleague supports them within that environment.

## Intended outcome

After the session, a new joiner should be able to explain:

- What Manafa does and the two business lines it serves
- The difference between borrowers, investors and the SCF-specific roles of buyer, supplier and funder
- Manafa’s key milestones, regulatory position and national-scale programs
- The seven financing products and the common financing lifecycle
- Which departments Digital Business regularly works with
- How Digital Product is organized and what Digital Business owns
- Manafa’s platform strategy and the route from a Digital Factory demand to production
- Who is in the Digital Business team and which core tools they will use

## Slide structure

### Opening

1. **Welcome to Manafa**
   Digital Business onboarding.

### Chapter 1 — Manafa

2. **Manafa**
   Section break: company, business model and people.

3. **Manafa at a glance**
   A concise company definition and the core relationship between borrowers, Manafa and investors.

4. **Two business lines, different participants**
   Crowdlending: Borrower ↔ Manafa ↔ Investor. Supply Chain Financing: Buyer, Supplier (Borrower) and Funder.

5. **Company milestones**
   Founded in 2018; debt crowdfunding licence; Series A; Saudi Unicorns selection; Aramco and SEC SCF programs; SCF Regulatory Sandbox.

6. **Regulatory position**
   2022 debt crowdfunding licence and 2025 SCF Regulatory Sandbox permission from the Saudi Central Bank.

7. **Series A and Saudi Unicorns**
   SAR 106 million Series A led by STV and Wa’ed Ventures, alongside Manafa’s selection for the Saudi Unicorns Program.

8. **National-scale SCF programs**
   Aramco at FII8 in 2024 and Saudi Electricity Company at FII9 in 2025, presented together as important SCF milestones.

9. **Manafa today**
   Current public metrics: total financing, users, team members and partnerships. Revalidate before every cohort.

10. **Executive team**
    Abdulaziz Aladwani as Founder and CEO, with the confirmed executive team beneath him.

11. **Manafa companies and technology delivery**
    Manafa, Sukuk Capital, Abyan Capital and Manafa Technologies in Pakistan.

12. **Investors and strategic partners**
    STV, Wa’ed Ventures and the key public and strategic partners relevant to Manafa’s business.

### Chapter 2 — Business and products

13. **Business and products**
    Section break: products, lifecycle and digital channels.

14. **Financing products**
    One visual overview of all seven products: Invoice Financing, Letter of Guarantee, Purchase Order Financing, Working Capital, Payroll Financing, Real Estate Financing and Supply Chain Financing.

15. **Financing lifecycle**
    Onboard → Request → Assess → Approve → Fund → Service.

16. **Digital channels and users**
    Borrower and Investor channels for the core marketplace; Buyer, Supplier/Borrower and Funder channels for SCF; Embedded as an API-based distribution channel rather than a financing product.

### Chapter 3 — Organization and Digital Business

17. **Organization and Digital Business**
    Section break: departments, platforms, people and delivery.

18. **Manafa departments**
    The stakeholder departments a Digital Business joiner is likely to work with: Credit; Compliance, Risk & BCM; Legal & Governance; Business Operations; Portfolio Management; Collections; Customer Care; Technology & Engineering; Cybersecurity; Finance & Treasury; Human Resources; Business Development & Partnership; Marketing & Branding Communication; Digital Investors; Digital Experience; and Internal Audit.

19. **Digital Product organization**
    Product reporting to the CTO, with Abdullah leading Digital Business and Raghad leading Digital Investors and Digital Experience.

20. **Digital Business scope**
    Abdullah as Product Director; responsibility for borrower, buyer, funder and embedded channels, plus the business-facing and back-office products supporting Manafa’s financing lines.

21. **Manafa Platform Strategy**
    The current five-hub strategy from the Product Leadership material:
    - Customer Hub: CRM, Client Lifecycle Management, Customer Service, Engagement Management and Transaction Management
    - Lending Hub: Loan Origination, Loan Management and Collection
    - Financial Hub: Ledger, Treasury, Invoice Management and Tax
    - Embedded Hub: Developer Platform, ERP Middleware and API Platform
    - Shared Foundations: Identity and Access Management, Log Management and Knowledge Management

22. **From demand to production**
    Digital Factory demand → Discovery → Confluence BRD when structured depth is needed → linked Jira delivery in Borrower, Embedded, Admin or Investor projects → Production validation and learning.

23. **Digital Business team**
    Abdullah and the current Product Leads, Business Analysts and UX/UI Designers on one team slide, using portraits where approved and restrained placeholders otherwise.

24. **How we work**
    Direct communication, cross-functional delivery, documented decisions and early ownership with guidance and feedback.

25. **Tools and company essentials**
    Slack, Microsoft 365, Jira, Confluence and the HR system, plus leave requests, attendance and the professional certificate-support material.

### Closing

26. **Thank you**
    Questions and welcome to Digital Business.

## Maintenance before each cohort

Reconfirm these items before presenting:

- Public metrics on Manafa’s website
- Executive titles and portraits
- Digital Business team names and titles
- Department names
- Product taxonomy and business-line terminology
- Platform ownership and Platform Strategy
- Digital Factory, Confluence and Jira workflow
- Regulatory wording and dates
- Official photographs, logos and links
- HR system name, leave process and current certificate-support documents

## Material intentionally kept outside this presentation

The following belong in the training path, practical assignments or Buddy Program:

- The seven-month learning and contribution roadmap
- Specific BA and UX/UI courses and completion targets
- Detailed professional-certification recommendations
- Staging access instructions and test data
- Seven-product creation, credit-assessment, investment and closure exercises
- First-week and first-month task plans
- Buddy selection, responsibilities, check-ins and escalation guidance
- Performance assessment or employment-conversion decisions
